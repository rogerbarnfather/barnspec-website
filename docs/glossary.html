Glossary

One word, one meaning.

Every term Barnspec uses, in alphabetical order. A word means one thing across this whole site, and matches the words in the browser app and on the command line.

Action
What a role does, in words that finish “they will now…”: book a room for three nights. An action is a building block, defined once and reused. It can take parameters.
Area of business
A distinct part of what the business does, named in your own words with a meaning that says what it covers. It holds roles, building blocks and Phases. Never shortened.
Barn
A project with a .barn/ folder at its root, where Barnspec keeps its records.
Building block
A subject, fact, action or parameter: the shared words a Scenario is written in, each defined once and reused.
Colour
A Scenario or story’s fulfilment: yellow, red or green, or none. Worked out from tickets and runs, never set by hand.
Elaboration
The way a released story changes: the story is opened in the Phase being built now, where Scenarios are added, amended or retired and its wording can change. It is merged into the story when that Phase is released.
Example
A sample value for a parameter, shown beside its meaning in the glossary. It can be set or removed at any time.
Fact
Something that can be true of a subject, such as is available. Scenarios use facts in their Given and Then. A fact is a building block and can take parameters.
Goal
What a story’s role wants to do. It finishes “wants to…”.
Impact
Whether a released story delivered its reward, recorded per Phase the story shipped in as evaluating, achieved, none or superseded. It never changes the story’s colour.
Outcome
Whether a Scenario’s action is expected to be accepted or rejected.
Parameter
A detail a fact or action takes, with a type. Each Scenario gives it a value, and it can carry an example.
Phase
A slice of work: a small, whole piece of value built end to end. An area of business has one being built and any number released. Releasing locks it.
Port
A small class, generated and then filled in by you, that connects one word of a Scenario to your software so it can be run: to set up a fact, check a fact, perform an action or sign in as a role.
Release
The one lock point of a Phase. It happens only when every story in the Phase is proven, and afterwards its stories change only by elaboration.
Reward
Why a story matters to its role. It finishes “so they…”.
Role
A kind of person within an area of business, with a meaning that says what they are responsible for. Stories belong to roles.
Run
Running a Scenario against your software. A passing run is the only thing that turns a Scenario green, and it is recorded until a later run replaces it.
Scenario
One concrete case of a story: given facts that hold at the start, when the role takes an action and expects it to be accepted or rejected, then facts that hold afterwards.
Stage
Where in a role’s dealings with the business a story sits: establish, adjust, transact, resolve or close. Optional. A story without one is Unassigned.
Story
What a role wants to do and why, told in the third person: the role wants to goal, so they reward. It belongs to one Phase.
Subject
A thing the business reasons about, such as a room or a booking. It has a meaning and holds facts. A subject is a building block.
Ticket
A unit of work tracked against one Scenario, with a title, a description and a status of to-do, in-progress or done. Tickets are disposable.
