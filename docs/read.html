Read what you captured

See the same specification as a map, documents, a glossary and a history.

Nothing in this page changes what you captured. Each command reads it and shows it another way. The browser app has the same views.

List and browse #

$ vendor/bin/barnspec show
Barn: …/hotel
reservations — booking and keeping rooms
$ vendor/bin/barnspec show reservations
Barn: …/hotel
reservations — booking and keeping rooms

  role: guest — choosing and securing somewhere to stay
    story: wants to book a room for a run of nights, so that they have somewhere to stay [transact]
      phase: first-bookings
      scenario: book-a-room-for-three-nights
    story: wants to cancel their booking, so that they are not held to a stay they will not take [resolve]
      phase: first-bookings

  subject: room — a room the hotel lets to guests
    fact: is-available — no booking holds the room on those nights

  subject: booking — a guest's hold on a room for a run of nights
    fact: is-confirmed — holds the room for the guest on these nights

  action: book a room for three nights
      parameter: nights (integer) — how many nights to stay

Add --json for the same outline as JSON, for scripts.

The story map #

Every story once, as it reads now, under its stage and grouped by role. A story whose Phase is not released yet is marked planned, and a story changed by a released elaboration shows its new wording.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec map --area-of-business=reservations
Barn: …/hotel
Story map — reservations

Establish
  (no stories)

Adjust
  (no stories)

Act
  The guest
    - wants to book a room for a run of nights, so they have somewhere to stay (planned)

Follow Up
  The guest
    - wants to cancel their booking, so they are not held to a stay they will not take (planned)

Terminate
  (no stories)

--role (repeatable) keeps only those roles, and --subject keeps only stories with a Scenario whose Given or Then is about that subject.

A document for each story #

One Markdown file and one HTML page per story, with its goal, reward and every Scenario’s given, when and then.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec write reservations --output-dir=docs/stories
Barn: …/hotel
Wrote: docs/stories/book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights.md
Wrote: docs/stories/book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights.html
Wrote: docs/stories/cancel-their-booking.md
Wrote: docs/stories/cancel-their-booking.html

The glossary #

Every role, subject, fact and action in an area of business, with its meaning, exactly as written and sorted so a word can be found at a glance. Nothing is summarised or made up. Parameters show their example beside their meaning.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec glossary reservations --output=docs/glossary.md
Barn: …/hotel
Wrote: docs/glossary.md
$ cat docs/glossary.md
# Glossary: reservations

This area of business focusses on: booking and keeping rooms

## Roles

- **guest** — choosing and securing somewhere to stay

## Subjects

### booking

a guest's hold on a room for a run of nights

- **is-confirmed** — holds the room for the guest on these nights

### room

a room the hotel lets to guests

- **is-available** — no booking holds the room on those nights

## Actions

- book a room for three nights
  - nights (integer) — how many nights to stay

To write every area of business at once, leave the area of business out and give a folder instead. This writes a glossary for each, plus an index linking them.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec glossary --output-dir=docs/glossary
Barn: …/hotel
Wrote: docs/glossary/glossary-reservations.md
Wrote: docs/glossary/glossary-reservations.html
Wrote: docs/glossary/index.md
Wrote: docs/glossary/index.html

The history #

The story map Phase by Phase: columns are the five stages (plus Unassigned when anything sits there), rows are Phases with the newest at the top. It is written as linked HTML pages.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec history --output-dir=docs/history
Barn: …/hotel
Wrote: docs/history/index.html
Wrote: docs/history/reservations.html

Each card links to its story document and to the glossary. --stories-dir and --glossary-dir say where those are, relative to the history folder; the defaults are ../stories and ../glossary.

Release notes #

A released Phase’s stories, in the wording they had then, with their Scenarios, as Markdown to copy.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec release-notes --area-of-business=reservations --phase="first bookings"
Barn: …/hotel
The guest wants to book a room for a run of nights, so they have somewhere to stay:
  - book a room for three nights
