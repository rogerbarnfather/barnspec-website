Capture stories and Scenarios

Write stories and Scenarios down, and change them until their Phase is released.

Capture a story #

In the browser app, New story asks who wants something, what they want to do, why, and where it sits, with the sentence building up as you answer. On the command line:

$ vendor/bin/barnspec create-story --area-of-business=reservations \
    --area-of-business-meaning="booking and keeping rooms" \
    --phase="first bookings" --phase-meaning="guests can book a room" \
    --role=guest --role-meaning="choosing and securing somewhere to stay" \
    --goal="book a room for a run of nights" --reward="have somewhere to stay" \
    --stage=transact
Barn: …/hotel
Story created:
  area of business: reservations (created)
  role: guest (created)
  story: book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights (created)
  stage: transact
  story id: 01a1cb3e-…

An area of business, role or Phase that already exists is reused: give its title, and leave out the -meaning flags, which are only needed for something new. --phase and --phase-meaning apply only when the area of business is new; they default to “Phase 1”. --stage is optional and is one of establish, adjust, transact, resolve or close.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec create-story --area-of-business=reservations --role=guest \
    --goal="cancel their booking" --reward="are not held to a stay they will not take" \
    --stage=resolve
Barn: …/hotel
Story created:
  area of business: reservations (existing)
  role: guest (existing)
  story: cancel-their-booking (created)
  stage: resolve
  story id: 01a1cb3e-…

An area of business on its own #

To make an area of business and its first Phase before any story, and to add the next Phase later:

vendor/bin/barnspec create-area-of-business --title=Laundry --meaning="washing and returning linen" \
    --phase="first loads" --phase-meaning="linen goes out and comes back"
vendor/bin/barnspec create-phase --area-of-business=laundry --title="..." --meaning="..."

Note. A new Phase can only be made once the active one is released. See Phases, releasing and changing.

Capture a Scenario #

In the browser app, New story offers to write the story’s happy path straight away, and + New Scenario on a story starts at the When. Nothing is saved until you save, and any new building block you describe on the way is created only then.

On the command line there are two ways. refine is a wizard for a person at a terminal. It asks which area of business, role and story, then walks through the Scenario one question at a time, offering to reuse each existing subject, fact and action or to make a new one.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec refine
Barn: …/hotel

Select an area of business:

[0] - reservations
[1] - Quit

>

For a script, an AI agent or a seed file, create-scenario takes the whole Scenario as JSON, so nothing is interactive. Subjects, facts, actions and parameters are found or created as refine would; only the area of business, role and story must already exist.

{
    "area-of-business": "reservations",
    "role": "guest",
    "story": "book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights",
    "actionSentence": "book a room for three nights",
    "actionParameters": {
        "nights": {"meaning": "how many nights to stay", "type": "integer", "value": 3}
    },
    "given": [
        {
            "subject": "room",
            "subjectMeaning": "a room the hotel lets to guests",
            "fact": "is-available",
            "factMeaning": "no booking holds the room on those nights"
        }
    ],
    "outcome": "accepted",
    "then": [
        {
            "subject": "booking",
            "subjectMeaning": "a guest's hold on a room for a run of nights",
            "fact": "is-confirmed",
            "factMeaning": "holds the room for the guest on these nights"
        }
    ]
}
$ vendor/bin/barnspec create-scenario --file=scenario.json
Barn: …/hotel
Scenario saved: 01a1cb3e-…
KeyMeaning
area-of-business, role, storyWhere the Scenario goes, by slug. They must already exist.
actionSentenceThe action, in words that finish “they will now…”.
actionParametersDetails the action takes: each has a meaning, a type and a value for this Scenario.
given, thenLists of facts. Each names a subject and fact, with subjectMeaning and factMeaning when they are new, and parameters if the fact takes any.
outcomeaccepted or rejected: what the role expects to happen.

A fact with no parameters is written the same way with parameters left out.

Change what you captured #

Until a Phase is released, everything in it can change, because the first wording is rarely the right one. Once it is released, a story changes only by elaboration.

Reword #

Reword a role, story, subject, fact, action, parameter or Phase. Give the new title or meaning, or a story’s new goal or reward.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec reword --area-of-business=reservations --story=book-a-room \
    --goal="book a room for a run of nights"
Barn: …/hotel
Reworded story 'book-a-room' of role 'guest':
  goal was: book a room
  goal now: book a room for a run of nights

Restage #

$ vendor/bin/barnspec restage-story --area-of-business=reservations \
    --story=book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights --stage=transact
Barn: …/hotel
Restaged book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights: transact

Add --clear instead of --stage to return a story to Unassigned.

Delete #

Delete a story with its Scenarios, a single Scenario, or a subject, fact, action or parameter that no Scenario uses. Barnspec says what it would delete and does nothing until you agree; pass --yes to agree without being asked.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec delete --area-of-business=reservations \
    --story=book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights
Barn: …/hotel
This deletes story 'book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights' of role 'guest'.
Nothing was deleted. Pass --yes to go ahead without being asked.

Warning. Deleting cannot be undone. A building block that a Scenario uses can be reworded but not deleted, and a subject can be deleted once it has no facts.

Examples #

A parameter can carry an example, shown on the glossary. It changes nothing that was released, so it can be set at any time, even after release. An empty example removes it.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec set-example --area-of-business=reservations \
    --action=book-a-room-for-three-nights --parameter=nights --example=3
Barn: …/hotel
Set the example of parameter 'nights' of action 'book-a-room-for-three-nights':
  example was: (none)
  example now: 3
