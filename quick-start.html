Quick start

From an empty project to a story on the map.

You need PHP 8.5 or later and Composer. Every command on this page was run in a new folder, with a hotel as the example, and the output shown is what it printed. It takes about ten minutes.

Install #

Barnspec is three Composer packages. barnspec is the library, barnspec-cli provides the vendor/bin/barnspec command, and barnspec-gui adds the browser app. From your project’s root:

$ composer require --dev rmb32/barnspec rmb32/barnspec-cli rmb32/barnspec-gui

Make a barn #

Barnspec keeps what you capture in a folder called .barn/ at the root of the project. init makes it.

$ vendor/bin/barnspec init
Made a barn: …/hotel
Barnspec keeps its spec in: …/hotel/.barn/barnspec

Commit .barn/ with your code: it is the specification. Barnspec finds it from any folder inside the project.

Capture a story #

A story says who wants something and why, in one sentence: the role wants to goal, so they reward. Roles belong to an area of business, a distinct part of what the business does. The first story creates the area of business, its role and its first Phase (a slice of work).

$ vendor/bin/barnspec create-story --area-of-business=reservations \
    --area-of-business-meaning="booking and keeping rooms" \
    --phase="first bookings" --phase-meaning="guests can book a room" \
    --role=guest --role-meaning="choosing and securing somewhere to stay" \
    --goal="book a room for a run of nights" --reward="have somewhere to stay" \
    --stage=transact
Barn: …/hotel
Story created:
  area of business: reservations (created)
  role: guest (created)
  story: book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights (created)
  stage: transact
  story id: 01a1cb3e-…

The stage says where in a guest’s dealings with the hotel the story sits. It is optional. A second story reuses the same area of business and role, so it only needs the new words:

$ vendor/bin/barnspec create-story --area-of-business=reservations --role=guest \
    --goal="cancel their booking" --reward="are not held to a stay they will not take" \
    --stage=resolve
Barn: …/hotel
Story created:
  area of business: reservations (existing)
  role: guest (existing)
  story: cancel-their-booking (created)
  stage: resolve
  story id: 01a1cb3e-…

IDs are shortened here with “…”; yours will be longer.

Add a Scenario #

A Scenario pins a story down with one concrete case: given how things stand, when the role does something, then what holds afterwards. Save this as scenario.json:

{
    "area-of-business": "reservations",
    "role": "guest",
    "story": "book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights",
    "actionSentence": "book a room for three nights",
    "actionParameters": {
        "nights": {"meaning": "how many nights to stay", "type": "integer", "value": 3}
    },
    "given": [
        {
            "subject": "room",
            "subjectMeaning": "a room the hotel lets to guests",
            "fact": "is-available",
            "factMeaning": "no booking holds the room on those nights"
        }
    ],
    "outcome": "accepted",
    "then": [
        {
            "subject": "booking",
            "subjectMeaning": "a guest's hold on a room for a run of nights",
            "fact": "is-confirmed",
            "factMeaning": "holds the room for the guest on these nights"
        }
    ]
}
$ vendor/bin/barnspec create-scenario --file=scenario.json
Barn: …/hotel
Scenario saved: 01a1cb3e-…

Each new word, such as room or is-available, is given its meaning the first time it appears and is reused from then on.

Note. For a person at a keyboard, vendor/bin/barnspec refine asks for the same things one question at a time. The browser app does too.

The map lists every story once, under the stage it sits at. Both stories read “planned” because their Phase has not been released. To turn each story into a readable document:

$ vendor/bin/barnspec write reservations --output-dir=docs/stories
Barn: …/hotel
Wrote: docs/stories/book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights.md
Wrote: docs/stories/book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights.html
Wrote: docs/stories/cancel-their-booking.md
Wrote: docs/stories/cancel-their-booking.html
$ cat docs/stories/book-a-room-for-a-run-of-nights.md
# book a room for a run of nights

**The** guest
**Wants to** book a room for a run of nights
**So they** have somewhere to stay

**Stage** transact — The role completes the exchange the area of business exists for: pays, places the order, submits, publishes.

## Scenarios

### book a room for three nights

**Given**
- no booking holds the room on those nights

**When**
- they book a room for three nights (nights=3) — expected to be accepted

**Then**
- holds the room for the guest on these nights

Open the browser app #

$ vendor/bin/barnspec gui

This serves the barn on your own machine and opens it in your browser; stop it with Ctrl-C. It lists the areas of business, and opening reservations shows the same story map with a card for each story. The browser app page covers the rest.
